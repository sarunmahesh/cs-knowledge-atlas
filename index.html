EDITORIAL STANDARD

How evidence is treated

Primary sources first

Prefer original publications, academic research, official standards and published organisational handbooks.

Claims ≠ proof

Vendor recommendations and practitioner experience are labelled. Outcomes require baselines, methods and corroboration.

Continuous review

Every entry records source, date or year, category, evidence type, review status and limitations.
