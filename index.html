06 / EDITORIAL METHOD

Evidence standards

Open contribution does not mean open publication. Every research claim is assessed for source fidelity, methodological soundness and appropriate uncertainty.

01

Source identity

Capture original publisher, author, date, URL or DOI and where the relevant evidence appears.

02

Claim fidelity

Confirm the cited material supports the specific statement, rather than merely mentioning the same subject.

03

Methods & limitations

Distinguish experiments, surveys, vendor cases, practitioner experience and untested hypotheses.

04

Independent review

Flag conflicts, disclosure gaps, missing data and duplicated evidence. Editorial approval is explicit.

Research publication states

Submitted

Untrusted contribution; not part of approved knowledge.

Source identified

Original source can be located; content has not been fully evaluated.

Under review

Bibliographic, claim-level and methodology checks ongoing.

Approved

Editor has approved specified claims with recorded scope and limitations.

Contested

Material contradictions or correction issues need investigation.

An agent cannot self-certify its output as approved. A schema validation pass is not scientific validation.
